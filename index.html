I am a Research Scientist at VUNO Inc., where I focus on 3D computer vision for lung CT analysis. Before joining VUNO, I was a Graduate Research Assistant at the POSTECH Computer Vision Lab, where I earned my M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering under Professor Minsu Cho. My research interests lie primarily in computer vision and deep learning, with a particular focus on learning visual correspondence and its applications. If you're interested in my research, feel free to contact me via email or visit my GitHub and LinkedIn.
